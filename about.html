About Us

Jen and Garth have been making things together since 2012, when a half-baked plan to build a disco dance floor accidentally introduced them to each other. They've been collaborating ever since, building things that are useful, beautiful, delicious, or just genuinely fun to make. They married in 2017 where they finally built that disco dance floor and it became the focal point of their wedding. It still makes an appearance from time to time at events. Tiger Town is their San Francisco home lab, and this is a catalog of all their ongoing projects.

JEN

Multidisciplinary creative and former small business owner. She's led creative teams building brand identities, large-scale installations, and websites, and has spent just as many hours redesigning an event show booth from scratch because the first version was almost right. She's meticulously organized, instinctively modular, and thinks you're crazy if you don't use the scientific method approach to recipe making. She also makes a killer vegan ice cream.

GARTH

Software engineer by day, maker by night. He runs a workshop out of their garage with more than one 3D printer, a coffee roaster, and enough tools to build almost anything. His measure-twice-cut-once discipline means nearly everything he does is meticulously crafted. He's the kind of guy you want during an apocalypse but he's calm under pressure and can MacGyver just about anything. He also roasts a pretty good bag o' beans.
