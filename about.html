About Us

We've been making things together since 2012, when a half-baked plan to build a disco dance floor accidentally introduced us to each other. We've been collaborating ever since, building things that are useful, beautiful, delicious, or just plain fun to make. We got married in 2017 when we finally built that disco dance floor and it became the focal point of our wedding. It still comes out at events from time to time. Tiger Town is what we call our little home lab, and this is a portfolio of our ongoing projects.

JEN

Multidisciplinary creative and former small business owner. She's led creative teams building brand identities, large-scale installations, and websites, and has spent just as many hours redesigning an event show booth from scratch because the first version was almost right. She's meticulously organized, instinctively modular, and thinks you're crazy if you don't use the scientific method approach to recipe making. She also makes a killer vegan ice cream.

GARTH

His measure-twice-cut-once discipline means nearly everything he does is meticulously crafted. He's the kind of guy you want during an apocalypse because he's calm under pressure and can MacGyver just about anything. He also roasts a pretty good bag o' beans.
